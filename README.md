# Project Background
Research on (**Republic of Chad**) government budget allocation helping audience understand governments public funds. Includes collecting data, analysing and generating insights. Data can be viewed in this [link](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1jziRy2r3Ospl8jHQamnZoHrB-4qBXxY2vX7xyrI1aB8/edit?usp=sharing)

# Project Objective
-Help audiences understand how governments allocate public funds and where gaps often emerge.

# Executive summary
# Insights

### Education

-_National Education and Civic Promotion_: Has an insignificant difference of extra 7,52 billion CFA Francs (0,78%) expenditure spent from budgeted amount of 91,92 billion (9,41%) and actual spending of 99,53 billion (10,19%)

-_Higher Education, Scientific Research and Vocational Training_: Less spending from budgeted amount being a difference of 3,13 billion CFA Francs (0,32%) and budgeted amount being 32,53 billion (3,33%) whilst actual being 29,40 billion (3,01%)

-Education sector indicates priority in government spending as it has the highest percentage of overall expenditure of 10,19%

### Healthcare
-_Public Health and Prevention_: The highest budgeted sector with 95,14 billion CFA Francs (9,74%) budget and actual expenses being less by 63,30 billion (6,48%), indicating underspending of 31,84 billion (3,26%)

-Health care sector being the secondary primary government spending with high percentage of overall expenditure of 6,48% indicating

### Infrastructure and Utilities
-_Water and Electricity_: Budget amount of 93,48 billion CFA Francs (9,57%) with lower actual spent amount of 23,44 billion CFA Francs (2,4%), leading to a difference of 70,04 billion (7,17%) left to be spent

### Social Protection
-_Social Action, Solidarity and Humanitarian Affairs_: Budget amount is 10,45 billion CFA Francs (1,07%) and was exceeded by the actual spending amount having to be an amount of 45,62 billion (4,67%), resulting to difference of 35,17 billion (3,60%) extra spent expenditure

-_Women and Early Childhood Development_: Lowest budget of 3.91 billion CFA Francs (0,4%) expenditure and having an actual spent expenditure amount of 3,81 billion (0,39%), spending difference being less than 0,10 billion (0,01%)

### Economic Development
-_Agricultural Production and Industrialization_: Budget of 31,55 billion CFA Francs (3,23%) and actual of 22,86 billion (2,34%) spent being less expenditure of 8,69 billion (0,89%) from budget

-_Livestock Farming and Animal Production_: A minor difference between budgeted amount and actual amount, budgeted being 11,14 billion CFA Francs (1,14%) and actual spending being 9,28 billion (0,95%) with difference of less 1,86 billion (0,19%) spending from budget

### Social Development
-_Youth and Sports_: A difference of less expenditure of 0,59 billion CFA Francs (0,06%) from budget with the budget having 7,42 billion CFA Francs (0,76%) and actual being 6,84 billion (0,7%).

### Environment and Sustainability
-_Environment, Fisheries and Sustainable Development_: An overspending of budgeted amount with difference of 3,22 billion CFA Francs (0,33%) from budgeted amount of 33,11 billion (3,39%) whilst actual being 36,34 (3,72%)

-Environmental and Sustainability sector having the lowest percentage in overall expenditure suggests government not prioritizing spending in such sector
